Scenario: an HR chatbot answers questions about this policy:

  ACME leave policy: full-time staff get 14 days of annual leave per year. Unused leave can be carried over, up to 5 days, into the next year. Sick leave is separate: 10 days per year with a medical certificate for absences longer than 2 days. Remote work is allowed up to 3 days a week.

We ask 8 questions. 5 are answerable from the policy; 3 are NOT, so a good bot
must say "I don't know" instead of inventing something.
Same model (bonsai-2:latest) both times -- only the prompt changes. A judge model (bonsai-2:latest) grades every answer:
  Correct   right answer, or an honest "I don't know"
  Grounded  only facts from the policy, nothing invented
  Relevant  answers the question, no padding or extra tips

▶ Step 1 -- save the questions to Phoenix as a dataset
  dataset policy-qa (8 questions)

▶ Step 2 -- save the two prompts to Phoenix as versions of one prompt
  Prompt 1 (first draft): "You are a friendly HR assistant. Answer the employee's question helpfully and completely, adding any useful tips."
  Prompt 2 (the fix):     "Answer using ONLY the CONTEXT. Be brief: one or two sentences. If the context does not contain the answer, reply exactly: I don't know."

▶ Step 3 -- ask the bot every question with each prompt, and grade the answers
  Prompt 1 ... done.  Prompt 2 ... done.
